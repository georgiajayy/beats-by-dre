# Beats by Dre — Creative Strategy & Audience Research

A portfolio case study documenting a three-part research and creative strategy project exploring how Beats by Dre can translate cultural influence into stronger consumer connection.

## Project question

**How can Beats remain culturally influential while making its advertising feel more authentic, relatable and persuasive?**

## Workstreams

1. **Competitive Creative Intelligence** — review of 25 headphone advertisements: 10 Beats and 15 competitor executions.
2. **Audience & Consumer Research** — evaluation of four advertisements using 21 valid responses from 24 submissions.
3. **Creative Strategy Framework** — platform playbook, creative standards, evaluation rubric and strategic recommendations.

## Central strategic thesis

Beats has mastered cultural attention; the opportunity is to turn that attention into credibility and purchase consideration through more experience-led, platform-native creative.

**Core creative principle:** Culture should attract attention, not replace the product story.

## Research integrity

The findings are directional, based on the samples described in each project. They should inform further creative testing, not be treated as representative of the entire headphone market or as proof of sales impact. The Bose competitor observations are based on only three advertisements. The four-ad creative rubric remains pending assessment.
