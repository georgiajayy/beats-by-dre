# Presentation Outline — Beats by Dre: Turning Cultural Influence into Consumer Connection

## Big idea

Beats has mastered cultural attention; the opportunity is to turn that attention into credibility and purchase consideration through more experience-led, platform-native creative.

## Slide 1 — Can Beats turn attention into trust?

Introduce the brand's recognisable relationship with music, sport, celebrity and popular culture. Establish the question: does being noticed mean audiences believe in the product?

## Slide 2 — Research design

- 25 headphone advertisements reviewed: 10 Beats and 15 competitors.
- Four advertisements tested with 21 valid consumer responses.
- Evaluation focused on attention, believability and purchase consideration.

## Slide 3 — Celebrity talent attracts attention, but not necessarily credibility

- 50% of Beats advertisements featured celebrity talent, compared with 13.3% of competitor advertisements.
- Beats Celebrity attracted the most attention (8/21).
- 3/21 considered it the most believable.
- Average believability rating: 3.0, the lowest of the four adverts tested.

## Slide 4 — Athlete / Sport creative connected more strongly with credibility and purchase interest

Present the finding that Beats Athlete / Sport was considered the most believable and most likely to encourage purchase among the four tested advertisements.

## Slide 5 — Competitor creative suggests a direction to investigate

Show the Bose sample observation: 2/3 creator/influencer talent and 2/3 UGC-style. Explicitly qualify the small sample.

## Slide 6 — Strategic opportunity

Translate the evidence into three principles:

1. Culture should attract attention, not replace the product story.
2. Authenticity should be designed into the creative.
3. Platform relevance should not compromise brand identity.

## Slide 7 — Creative application

Introduce “More Than the Moment”, an athlete-focused short-form concept showing headphones as part of preparation and training.

## Slide 8 — Testing and next steps

Propose a controlled TikTok creative test using the same product and target audience:

- Version A: celebrity-led creative, focused on cultural influence.
- Version B: experience-led creative, featuring an athlete or relevant creator using the product in a real-world context.

Compare attention, brand recall, perceived authenticity, believability and purchase consideration. Views alone should not determine the winner.

## Presentation integrity

Distinguish observed research findings from strategic interpretation and proposed next steps. Do not present the creative concept as a completed campaign or the proposed test as having already taken place.
