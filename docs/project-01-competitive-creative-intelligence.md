# Project 01 — Competitive Creative Intelligence

## Objective

Understand how Beats by Dre and competing headphone brands use advertising creative to establish brand identity, capture attention and communicate product relevance.

## Method

Reviewed **25 headphone advertisements**:

- 10 Beats advertisements.
- 15 competitor advertisements, including Apple, Sony, Bose and JBL.

The review considered creative features including talent, hooks, platforms, production styles, duration and effectiveness.

## Findings

### Celebrity talent is more prominent in the Beats sample

| Measure | Beats | Competitors |
|---|---:|---:|
| Advertisements featuring celebrity talent | 5 of 10 (50%) | 2 of 15 (13.3%) |

This indicates a stronger reliance on celebrity talent within the Beats sample. It does not, by itself, establish whether celebrity creative is more or less effective.

### Directional observation from the Bose sample

- 2 of 3 Bose advertisements featured creator/influencer talent.
- 2 of 3 Bose advertisements used low-production, UGC-style content.

The Bose sample is small (n=3). Treat this as a prompt for further investigation, not a definitive statement about Bose's overall strategy or performance.

### Question-led hooks

No question-led opening hooks were identified in the 10 Beats advertisements reviewed (0/10).

## Strategic implications

- Celebrity recognition can help establish cultural relevance, but should not substitute for a clear product story.
- Creator-led and UGC-style executions are worth testing as a complementary strand.
- Opening hooks should be assessed deliberately, including whether a question, visual action or immediate product demonstration gives audiences a reason to continue watching.
- Platform, talent and production choices should be evaluated in relation to the intended audience and communication objective.

## Limitations

This is a 25-ad sample, not a census of the category. Competitor observations should be interpreted within the sample. In particular, Bose-specific observations are based on three advertisements only.
