# Creative Concept — More Than the Moment

## Format

A short-form TikTok advertising concept for Beats, centred on an athlete preparing for and completing a training session.

## Creative premise

Show how music and headphones form part of an athlete's preparation, focus and performance. The product should belong naturally in the experience rather than appearing only as a brand prop.

## Proposed sequence

1. **Opening:** Close-up of the athlete putting on their Beats headphones. On-screen question: “What gets you in the zone?”
2. **Training sequence:** Quick cuts between preparation, movement and training, with the headphones integrated naturally into the experience.
3. **Closing:** The athlete finishes the session. Product close-up and closing line: “Find your focus. Beats.”

## Strategic rationale

The concept translates the consumer research into a practical creative hypothesis. The Beats Athlete / Sport advertisement was found most believable and most likely to encourage purchase among the four advertisements tested. The proposed execution explores experience-led storytelling while retaining the cultural energy associated with Beats.
