## Known limitations

1. Project 01 is based on a sample of 25 advertisements and does not represent every campaign in the category.
2. The Bose observations are based on three advertisements only.
3. Project 02 has 21 valid responses. It is directional research, not a representative consumer study.
4. The findings identify associations and opportunities for testing; they do not establish causation or prove sales impact.
5. The creative evaluation rubric is a structured judgement framework. Its proposed pass threshold is not an industry standard.
6. The four advertisement rubric assessments are pending.
7. The “More Than the Moment” concept is documented as a proposal; no completed production or campaign performance is evidenced in the current project record.
