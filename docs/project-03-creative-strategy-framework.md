# Project 03 — Creative Strategy Framework

## Strategic challenge

**How can Beats remain culturally influential while making its advertising feel more authentic, relatable and persuasive?**

The research suggests an opportunity to strengthen the relationship between the attention Beats advertising generates and the credibility needed to influence consumer consideration.

## Central strategic thesis

Beats has mastered cultural attention; the opportunity is to turn that attention into credibility and purchase consideration through more experience-led, platform-native creative.

## Three creative principles

### 1. Culture should attract attention, not replace the product story

Use influential talent, music and cultural references to establish relevance, while ensuring the product has a meaningful role in the creative.

**Evidence:** Celebrity talent appeared in 5/10 Beats advertisements. In the consumer research, the Beats Celebrity advertisement attracted the most attention (8/21), but only 3/21 considered it the most believable.

### 2. Authenticity should be designed into the creative

Prioritise genuine experiences, credible talent and storytelling that demonstrates how the product fits into people's lives.

**Evidence:** The Beats Athlete / Sport advertisement was rated the most believable and most likely to encourage purchase among the four advertisements tested.

### 3. Platform relevance should not compromise brand identity

Adapt creative execution to platform behaviours while retaining recognisable Beats branding, visual identity and cultural positioning.

**Evidence:** The competitive sample included creator-led and UGC-style executions, including within the small Bose sample. This is a direction to test, not proof of superior performance.

## Framework components

The Creative Strategy Framework contains:

1. **Platform Playbook** — platform-specific principles for YouTube, TikTok and Meta, grounded in official platform guidance.
2. **Beats Creative Standards** — practical DO/DON'T principles for voice, look, sound and talent.
3. **Creative Evaluation Rubric** — a five-criterion assessment tool.
4. **Strategic Recommendations Summary** — translation of the research into creative direction.

## Creative Evaluation Rubric

Each advertisement is assessed against five criteria on a 1–5 scale:

| Criterion | Assessment question |
|---|---|
| Brand identity | Is the brand recognisable, distinctive and consistently represented? |
| Audience relevance | Does the creative connect with its intended audience, interests and cultural context? |
| Attention & hook | Does the opening capture attention and give audiences a reason to continue watching? |
| Authenticity & credibility | Does the message feel believable, relevant and supported by the execution? |
| Product integration | Is the product meaningfully incorporated into the story and its value communicated? |

**Maximum:** 25 points per advertisement.

**Proposed pass rule:** 18/25 or above overall, with at least 3/5 in every individual criterion.

The four advertisements awaiting assessment are Beats Celebrity, Beats Athlete / Sport, Samsung Everyday People and Skullcandy Music / Lifestyle. The rubric scores are currently pending. The threshold is a proposed internal benchmark, not an industry-standard accreditation, and a pass would not independently demonstrate commercial effectiveness or sales impact.

## Platform Playbook — scope

The Platform Playbook includes three core platform sections:

- **YouTube:** creative principles informed by Google's ABCDs of effective creative.
- **TikTok:** creative principles informed by TikTok Creative Codes and Creative Best Practices.
- **Meta:** creative principles informed by Instagram Reels features and creator best-practice guidance.

The documented guidance emphasises strong openings, clear communication, platform-native execution, purposeful use of music and editing, and testing hooks against retention and engagement.

## Research basis and caveat

The framework draws on Project 01 (25 advertisements) and Project 02 (21 valid responses). Findings are directional and should inform further creative testing, not be treated as universal consumer behaviour.
